# tiramisu recipe


## Preparation
- Chill the tiramisu for several hours before serving
- Prepare a strong espresso and let it cool
- Use a rectangular dish (about 20 × 30 cm)
- Have a hand mixer or whisk ready for the cream



## Ingredients
- 250 g mascarpone
- 200 ml heavy cream
- 80 g sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 200 ml strong espresso (cooled)
- 200–250 g ladyfingers (savoiardi)
- Cocoa powder for dusting
- Optional: 2–3 tbsp coffee liqueur (e.g. Amaretto or Kahlúa)



## Instructions
- Brew the espresso and let it cool
- Whip the heavy cream until stiff
- In another bowl, mix mascarpone, sugar, and vanilla until smooth
- Gently fold the whipped cream into the mascarpone mixture
- Quickly dip each ladyfinger into the espresso
- Arrange a layer of soaked ladyfingers in the dish
- Spread half of the mascarpone cream on top
- Add another layer of soaked ladyfingers
- Cover with the remaining mascarpone cream
- Dust the top with cocoa powder
- Refrigerate for at least 4 hours (preferably overnight) before serving
